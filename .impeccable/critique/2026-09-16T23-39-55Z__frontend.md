### Overall Impression
The marine analytics aesthetic gives the tool a much-needed specific identity, but it leans too heavily into decorative tech tropes (scanlines, background grids, thick left borders) that risk feeling like a sci-fi mockup rather than a serious operational tool. Cutting the decorative excess will elevate it from a cool demo to a professional dashboard.

### What's Working
- **Typography pairing**: Fira Code for data and Fira Sans for UI creates a strong, functional rhythm.
- **Color palette**: The deep sea (#030C14) background combined with radar accents creates a highly specific mood.
- **Component structure**: The sharp borders and target crosshairs successfully reinforce the "tracking" theme without breaking layout.

### Priority Issues
- **[P1] Artificial "Tech" Tropes**: The thick left borders (`border-l-4`) on StatCard and ErrorAlert are generic AI-UI slop.
  - **Why it matters**: It undermines the bespoke marine analytics feel by using a widely recognized generated pattern.
  - **Fix**: Remove `border-l-4` and rely on the sharp 1px borders and typography for hierarchy.
  - **Suggested command**: `/impeccable distill`

- **[P1] Extraneous Background Noise**: The full-page grid background in `index.css` is decorative.
  - **Why it matters**: True utility dashboards keep the background solid to maximize contrast for the actual data.
  - **Fix**: Remove the decorative grid background and keep the solid deep sea color.
  - **Suggested command**: `/impeccable quieter`

- **[P2] Scanline Overuse**: Scanlines on both video containers and image result overlays create visual noise.
  - **Why it matters**: When every component has a scanline, it becomes a gimmick rather than an active state indicator.
  - **Fix**: Restrict scanlines exclusively to `LoadingState.jsx` to indicate active processing, removing them from static results.
  - **Suggested command**: `/impeccable distill`

### Persona Red Flags
- **Alex (Power User)**: Forced to use mouse for drag-and-drop or clicking the upload area. No keyboard shortcut (e.g., `Cmd+U`) to quickly upload and scan.
- **Sam (Accessibility-Dependent User)**: The dark mode contrast is good, but the scanline animations might trigger motion sensitivity issues. No `prefers-reduced-motion` check for the CSS animations.

### Minor Observations
