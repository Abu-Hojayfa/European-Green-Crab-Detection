## What's Working

1. **Unified color system**: A single emerald accent for actions, teal for data, hue-tinted secondaries throughout. No more three-green confusion. Every text color is derived from the background hue, not default gray.
2. **Flat surface structure**: The nested-card antipattern is fully eliminated. Content sections breathe directly on the page surface. The dropzone is a clear interactive affordance without being boxed inside another box.
3. **Complete interactive states**: Every interactive element has hover, focus-visible, and disabled states. The custom range slider, styled scrollbar, and themed selection color show browser-surface craft that most implementations skip entirely.

## Priority Issues

### [P2] Typography lacks distinctiveness — flagged by detector

**What**: Both Plus Jakarta Sans and Space Grotesk are flagged as overused AI-tool fonts. The detector found 3 instances. These are well-crafted typefaces, but their ubiquity in AI-generated UIs means they signal "template" rather than "authored."

**Why it matters**: For a marine conservation tool used by field researchers, the typography should project scientific authority and purpose-built precision — not "another Vercel starter kit."

**Fix**: Replace Plus Jakarta Sans with a less saturated body face. Consider **Outfit** (geometric but less common), **Manrope** (clean neo-grotesque with character), or **General Sans** (by Indian Type Foundry, available via Fontshare — free, self-hostable, and distinctive). Keep Space Grotesk only if it genuinely fits the brand, or consider **JetBrains Mono** for a technical/scientific display face.

**Suggested command**: `/impeccable typeset`

---

### [P2] Empty page state has excessive void below CTA

**What**: On desktop at 1440x900, the content ends at roughly 60% of the viewport height. Below the "Secure pipeline" trust note, there's 300+ pixels of empty dark space before the footer. The page feels incomplete or unfinished.

**Why it matters**: First-time users may think the page didn't fully load, or that they're missing content below the fold. The large void undermines the sense of a polished, complete product.

**Fix**: Either (a) vertically center the entire content block within the viewport using `min-h-[calc(100vh-header)]` with flex centering, so the upload area sits at the natural eye line, or (b) add a subtle ambient background element (a faint radial gradient, a topographic-style contour pattern at very low opacity) to give the empty space purpose.

**Suggested command**: `/impeccable layout`

---

### [P3] No first-time user guidance or empty-state personality

**What**: The page opens cold with "Analyse an image" and a dropzone. There's no explanation of what the tool does, what it will show, how it works, or why someone should trust it. The "Secure pipeline" note is technical and doesn't address the emotional question: "What will happen when I upload my field photo?"

**Why it matters**: Field researchers evaluating this tool for the first time need reassurance before uploading sensitive survey data. A brief one-line value proposition above the tabs ("Identify and count invasive European green crabs in your field images and videos") would anchor the purpose.

**Fix**: Add a single-line tagline between the header and tabs, or within the header itself. Not an eyebrow or kicker — just a short sentence of context for first-time visitors.

**Suggested command**: `/impeccable onboard`

---

### [P3] Confidence threshold has no explanation

**What**: In the ImageResult view, the confidence threshold slider appears with "Confidence: 50%" but no explanation of what this means for non-technical users.

**Why it matters**: A marine biologist seeing "Confidence: 50%" may not understand that lowering it will show more detections (with more false positives), or raising it shows fewer but higher-certainty detections. Without this context, the slider is inert for most users.

**Fix**: Add a single-line hint below the slider: "Lower values show more detections including uncertain ones. Higher values show only high-certainty detections."

**Suggested command**: `/impeccable clarify`

## Minor Observations

- The `hover:bg-accent-hover` on the CTA button is barely distinguishable from the resting `bg-accent` state in the screenshot — the color shift from `#34d399` to `#6ee7b7` is subtle on a dark background. Consider darkening the hover slightly instead of lightening, or adding a subtle shadow.
- The dropzone hover state (confirmed via screenshot) correctly shows a border color change to `accent/40`, which is visible but subtle. The icon doesn't change color on hover — adding `group-hover:text-accent` on the icon would strengthen the affordance.
- The "Privacy-first" badge in the header is hidden on mobile (`hidden sm:flex`). This is the right call for space, but mobile is where privacy concern is highest (users on personal phones).
- The footer text "Crab Vision · European Green Crab Detector" uses `text-ghost` which passes contrast for decorative text but is very faint. This is appropriate for a footer but worth monitoring.
